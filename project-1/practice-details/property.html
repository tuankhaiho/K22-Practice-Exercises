Tulsa Property Law Attorney

If you are facing a dispute over property, you may be feeling frustrated and unsure of your rights. Property disputes can affect your finances, your home, and your peace of mind as you deal with the legal and financial burdens that come with them. If you are dealing with a real estate or property matter, then you want an experienced and knowledgeable property law attorney by your side.

At The Rode Law Firm, a property law firm with locations in Tulsa and Sapulpa, Oklahoma, we are focused on protecting our clients' property rights and interests. We represent people in the Tulsa, Sapulpa, Broken Arrow, Sand Springs, and Owasso, Oklahoma areas who are facing property disputes by providing professional representation both in and out of the courtroom.

If you are involved in a property dispute, the steps you take afterward directly affect the outcome of your case. Keeping thorough records of deeds, titles, and agreements, and responding promptly to any notices or claims are prudent and highly recommended. Many issues, such as boundary disagreements or title defects, may build up over weeks or months before being resolved. Protecting your investment should be your number one priority.

Common Property Law Matters

Sometimes a property dispute becomes complicated because of disagreements between owners, neighbors, or tenants that may not be obvious right away, such as:

  • Boundary and easement disputes
  • Title defects and quiet title actions
  • Landlord-tenant disputes
  • Breach of real estate contracts
  • Zoning and land use disputes

At The Rode Law Firm, we will work with you to get you the best outcome possible for your case. We understand how stressful a property dispute can be, and how the effects of such can be life-altering. Our passion for helping clients protect what they've worked hard for makes us an excellent legal partner for you. We care about you and want to help you resolve your case fairly and quickly. With over 30 years of legal experience, we have the skills needed to effectively defend you as your property law attorney. You deserve a fair resolution for your property matter.

How We Will Handle Your Case?

When we meet with new clients, our priority is to establish a strong attorney-client relationship. This will help us collaborate with you throughout the entire legal process, and make you feel comfortable coming to us with any questions or concerns along the way. Communication is imperative and constant throughout your case. We'll speak at least once every three weeks so that both parties are never in the dark. As our client, you will receive a full list of do's and don'ts while we handle your legal situation. This will guide you on best practices so that we can get you the outcome you deserve.

The legal process can be confusing, so you may be wondering: how long will the process take? The answer to this question is dependent upon the nature of the property dispute, how the other party responds to your claim, and whether or not we need to go to trial. No matter what, your attorneys at The Rode Law Firm will be by your side to answer any questions or concerns as best we can.

What We Can Help You Resolve

If you are facing a property dispute, you have the right to protect your ownership interests and seek a fair resolution. We can often help you resolve:

  • Boundary line disputes
  • Quiet title and title defect claims
  • Easement and right-of-way disputes
  • Landlord-tenant disputes and evictions
  • Breach of purchase or sale agreements
  • Zoning variances and land use disputes
  • Adverse possession claims
  • Homeowners association disputes
  • Property partition actions
  • Real estate contract negotiations and more
